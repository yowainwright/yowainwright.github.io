## Working observations

These are interpretations of the selected prose. Check them against the actual
samples and the current draft rather than imposing a house style.

- Explain a small distinction until the reader can use it. The cookie, CLI,
  and dreams posts all build around differences that were easy to overlook.
- Ground an opinion in something experienced or observed when the draft supplies
  that material. The Zsh and employment posts begin from concrete experience.
- Let uncertainty and self-correction remain visible. The Node post leaves its
  forecast open; the personal essays acknowledge the author's own mistakes.
- Match emphasis to purpose. A question can probe a real concern, and repetition
  can clarify a difficult point. The same device need not appear in every draft.
- Keep ordinary vocabulary and conversational asides where natural. Technical
  prose can sound personal without adding a story or an emotional reaction.
- Preserve the draft's chosen degree of intimacy. The personal essays do not
  authorize adding intimate details, profanity, or autobiography to a tutorial.

The source posts contain dated claims, code, and typographical mistakes.
Use them for voice only. Do not treat publication dates as proof that text is
human-written, reproduce mistakes to imitate a person, or copy an old technical
claim into new writing without evidence.
